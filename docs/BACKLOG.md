# Backlog

Subjects waiting to be made, grouped by field. Each one has a hook that works for kids and a core idea for engineers.

**Suggested Season 1** (marked ★): six subjects from six different fields, all very visual, all things people have felt or seen without knowing why.

---

## Vibrations and waves

| | Phenomenon | Kids hook | Engineering core |
|---|---|---|---|
| ★ | **Resonance** ([started](../phenomena/01-resonance/)) | Why small pushes make a swing go so high | Forced damped oscillator, amplification 1/(2ζ) |
| ★ | **Aliasing (wagon wheel effect)** | Why car wheels seem to spin backwards in videos | Sampling theorem, Nyquist frequency |
| | Standing waves | Why a guitar string makes one note | Modes, nodes, boundary conditions |
| | Doppler effect | Why an ambulance siren changes pitch as it passes | Frequency shift, relative velocity |
| | Tuned mass damper | How a giant ball keeps a skyscraper still | Two-degree-of-freedom system, tuning |

## Mechanics and structures

| | Phenomenon | Kids hook | Engineering core |
|---|---|---|---|
| ★ | **Capstan effect** | How one person holds a huge ship with a rope around a post | Belt friction, T₂ = T₁ e^(μθ) |
| ★ | **Stress concentration** | Why paper tears easily once there is a small cut | Stress concentration factor, crack tips, drilled crack stoppers |
| | Buckling | Why a straw bends suddenly when you push on it | Euler critical load, slenderness |
| | I-beam shape | Why beams look like the letter I | Second moment of area, bending stiffness |
| | Triangles in structures | Why cranes and bridges are made of triangles | Truss rigidity, statically determinate structures |
| | Arches and the catenary | Why a hanging chain, flipped over, makes the strongest arch | Pure compression, catenary curve |
| | Gyroscopic precession | Why a spinning top does not fall | Angular momentum, torque, precession rate |
| | Centre of mass and tipping | Why a lorry tips over in a tight bend | Stability, rollover threshold |
| | Fatigue | Why a paperclip breaks after bending it many times | S-N curve, cyclic loading |
