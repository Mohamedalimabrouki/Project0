Fact-check notes: every figure used on screen was checked against these sources in September 2026. Figures that were corrected during the check: the Millennium Bridge mechanism (negative damping, not simply marching in step), about 60 tuned mass dampers (not 50), the 166-walker test was on the north span, and the Taipei 101 ball lags about a quarter cycle (it does not swing opposite to the tower).
